# UI polish audit — shell and dialogs (2026-11-14)

Scope: title bar, navigation, toolbar row, banner slot, step footer, every dialog/popup/toast in the shots.
Method: every dialog shot and one or more shots of each screen viewed (light, dark, Ukrainian, narrow1000), details checked on 2× crops, causes looked up in `modules/ui/src/main/java/ua/bookloom/ui/` and `theme.css`. Nothing in the repository was changed.
Paths below are relative to `modules/ui/src/main/`; `theme.css` is `resources/ua/bookloom/ui/theme.css`. Coordinates are in points of the 1440×900 window.

## Summary

1. The "How progress is counted" fold is an unstyled JavaFX default (grey gradient bar, square corners) on the Translating card and inside every progress dialog — the one control that looks like it comes from another app (D-01).
2. Dialog footers follow no single rule: Cancel/Close is ghost, outlined, red-outlined or primary depending on the dialog; one dialog puts the primary button on the left; the three-way leave guard has no primary at all (D-02, D-03, D-04).
3. Banner icons are text glyphs (`⚠`, `ℹ`, `i`) in a top-aligned label: they sit about 6 pt above the first text line, and the info mark is a tiny serif "i", while the sleep note beside them uses a proper icon (D-05).
4. The shell banners (welcome back, finished, cannot start) are about 120 pt tall for two short lines and a button, and their right edge sticks out 8–20 pt past the cards below (D-06, D-07).
5. The progress dialog's panes have no inner padding: "Follow the current step", "What it decides", "Steps" and the Simple/Detailed switch touch the pane borders (D-08).
6. XL dialogs do not fill their fixed height: Model calls and the provider test leave a third of the card blank; the test's figures column breaks "uncache/d" mid-word (D-09, D-10).
7. Every dialog has about 36 pt of empty space between its title and its first line, with no visible divider, and the cards hang 6 pt under the title bar instead of being centred (D-11, D-12).
8. Export complete / Replace files: long paths break mid-word, "Copy path" is off the first line, the closing paragraph is glued to the last rule (D-13).
9. Small alignment slips: nav labels of Projects/Settings 2 pt left of the step labels, the sleep note's × floats after the text, result chips sit above their row's baseline, Add-term columns differ by 3 pt (D-14…D-18).
10. Overall: the shell is calm, consistent and holds up in dark, Ukrainian and at 1000 pt; nothing is broken. The untidiness is concentrated in dialog footers, banner internals and the progress dialog, and most of it is a handful of shared causes.

Counts: High 1 · Medium 12 · Low 15 (28 items).

## Dark theme

- Info banners lose their tone: in dark the info background is almost the page colour, so "Welcome back" (140, 149, 151) and the Export info banner (149) read as empty outlined boxes; the warning banner keeps a clear brown tint. Low — `-color-info-bg` in the `.root.theme-dark` block; a slightly bluer/lighter value would restore the meaning. (likely)
- Dialog card vs. dimmed page (161, 163): the card's fill equals the cards behind the scrim and the footer band is barely different from the body, so the dialog separates from the page mostly by its shadow. Low — `.shell-scrim` opacity 0.45 on a dark page; a stronger scrim in dark would do. (likely)
- D-01 is also visible in dark (147): the default fold becomes a darker gradient strip with a hard frame.
- No light leftovers found in the shell, toolbar, nav, banners, chips or dialog cards; the connection chip, toggle and primary button keep their roles.

## Looks right — keep

- Title bar, navigation column and toolbar row share one geometry on every screen; the breadcrumb ("Workflow / Book Brief · step 2 of 5") is always in the same place and style.
- Navigation states: the active item (accent left bar, filled badge, bold label), done check marks and numbered badges are clear and aligned to one column.
- One primary button per toolbar, always rightmost, with the disabled reason written beside it ("Choose the languages in Book Brief", "Nothing is translated yet.").
- Destructive confirms are uniform: outlined Cancel + red-outlined action, same width, same wording pattern, the object named in the title.
- Dialog cards share radius, border, shadow, footer band and 18 pt side padding; three width classes only.
- Banner tones (warn / info / ok / err) use the same roles for the same meanings in the shell slot, on screens and inside dialogs.
- The connection chip and the provider footer say the same thing in two places without contradicting each other; the red "No AI model chosen" footer state is unmistakable.
- Tooltips: dark bubble, readable in both themes, wrapped at a sensible width (43).
- Toasts: icon, bold title, one line, close ×, consistent with the info role (13, 205).
- The 1000 pt title-bar priority and the shortened Back label.
