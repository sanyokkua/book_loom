# The Harbour

Mara walked to the harbour. The boats were gone. She waited there until it was completely dark.

The lighthouse keeper had not spoken to anyone for eleven years, and he liked it that way very much.

The wind rose over the water.

She counted 12 ships before the fog came in.

Tomas laughed at the old joke.

It was the winter of 1984.

Nobody else came to the pier that night.
