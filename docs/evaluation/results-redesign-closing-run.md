# Results: redesign closing run

*The closing run of `redesign-translation-pipeline` against the baseline, with the owner-accepted known limits.*

## Redesign closing run {#redesign-closing-run}

The success table of `redesign-translation-pipeline` group 16 (task 16.1, design D24). Every rule below is fixed before
any closing number exists; tasks 16.2 to 16.5 run the measurements and fill the `Result` column, task 16.6 judges each
row. A `Result` of "not run" is the red state. A failing quality row is named with the falsifier it meets and goes to
the owner with options; it is never reworded to pass.

**Method.**

- *Baselines.* The outputs of `79ffede6` recorded in task 1.10 (`#redesign-arms`, "Baselines of `79ffede6`"), kept under
  `modules/pipeline/src/test/resources/eval/baseline/`. The ru->uk book is paired with a fresh `79ffede6` run made in
  task 16.5, because the baselines hold invented text only.
- *Runs.* `gemma4:e4b-mlx` on Ollama, single-flight, K = 3 with the recorded seeds 1000, 1001, 1002 for the mini
  suites and `sequence` (K = 1 where a row says so); `gemma4:26b-mlx` once for the owner's en->uk book. Balanced unless
  a row says Max.
- *Statistics.* Unit = case (paragraph for `sequence` and the books); K repeats are averaged first. A real difference
  is the exact two-sided sign test on discordant units, p < 0.05: 6:0, 8:1, 10:2 and 15:4 are the smallest wins.
  Soft graders may not fall below baseline minus max(noise band, smallest detectable effect).
- *Judge.* `gemma4:26b`, local, pairwise with the order swapped, a win only when both orders agree. Its calibration
  failed the Wilson bounds (`#redesign-arms`, arm 1), so it is a screen only: every paragraph it marks as differing is
  read by the agent (with a Codex second opinion by hand), and that reading is the grade the rows below use.
- *Where things live.* Raw outputs, reports and the real books' text stay in the git-ignored
  `.temporary_context/redesign-closing/`. Committed: the invented-text outputs of the mini suites and `sequence`, and
  this table, which names each owner book and its hash as a measurement record (as the earlier sections of this file
  do). Real books, their text, their outputs and their reports are never committed.

Defects this run found and fixed in place: the stall watchdog ended a whole-book run in Proofread (`84deca31`), `Mr` matched inside `Mrs` in the window map (`3942d3e4`), and the window-injection grader misread a numbered line (`57190b05`).

Falsifiers met, and what the owner decided (2026-11-11): the four items below are accepted and recorded here as known limits of the redesigned pipeline; no follow-up change is planned for them.

- *Q1 (the new pipeline is not shown to be better than `79ffede6` on quality).* Accepted. On `sequence` at Balanced the new pipeline makes more calls than `79ffede6` (about 307 against 118) and takes about 1.4 times as long (about 1650 s against 1198 s of job time), and wins no significant quality margin; LTCR-1 (term consistency) improved from 0.9108 to 0.9682. Known limit: narrator gender slips are 9 (Balanced) and 5 (Max) against 0 in the old pipeline, flagged segments 12 against 9.
- *Known limit, narrator gender slips (from Q1).* Accepted. The narrator's gender can slip in verb forms of first-person chapters (9 slips at Balanced and 5 at Max on the 328-paragraph `sequence` book, against 0 before); the review desk and the audit's gender findings are the guard.
- *Known limit, source-language words (R8, the design's falsifier).* Accepted. 5 of 100 random ru->uk paragraphs kept a Russian content word (3 of 100 without the two Russian telegrams), above the 1 % line. e4b keeps a Russian word where the Ukrainian differs only slightly, and no check reads single words (the language-identity check reads a whole paragraph, the letter check only ы э ъ ё); the review desk is the guard.
- *Known limit, names pass: possessives and case forms stored as separate glossary rows (R3, R4 and the hand-run finding D7).* Accepted. A possessive stored as its own row is read as another character and can raise a false name-swap block, and case forms with their own targets split a name into two renderings (7 of the top 20 names had two spellings in the ru->uk book; on the en book the names pass gave short-name rows with an empty Forms column). In the ru->uk book this led to one flagged paragraph (1 of 1280, 182 words) that export wrote in Russian: the draft had one stray letter of another script, the one repair removed it but the false name-swap blocks stayed, and export then re-checked the stored refused reply (the draft) and fell back to the source. The review desk is the guard; no code change and no follow-up change is planned.
- *R2 (20 letters outside the alphabet, 19 of them in the R3 paragraph) and R6 (24:16, p = 0.268).* R2 follows R3; R6 favours the new pipeline without significance on one reader and 250 of 747 differing paragraphs.
