**This file intentionally holds no rules.** `AGENTS.md` is the single source of truth for BookLoom's operating
manual — the loop, the Definition of Done, the git protocol, the non-negotiables with their enforcement, and
what will bite you. The `@AGENTS.md` line above imports it, so everything in it applies here verbatim.

If you are about to add a rule, a convention, or an invariant to this file: **put it in `AGENTS.md` instead.** Two
copies drift, and a drifted operating manual is worse than one that lives somewhere slightly less obvious.
